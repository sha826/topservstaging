Most agency case studies are adjectives in a trench coat — "boosted visibility," "improved presence." This one has numbers, because we tracked them from day one. Here's what actually happened when Flow Pros Plumbing came to us in September 2024.

## The starting line

When Flow Pros partnered with TopServ on **September 19th, 2024**, their online presence was minimal: roughly **1,000 estimated website visits a month** and a Google Business Profile that wasn't earning its keep. In a 15-mile service radius full of homeowners searching for plumbers every day, they were invisible for most of the searches that mattered.

## What we ran, in order

**1. The audit.** Full teardown of the website and Google Business Profile — structure, coverage, metadata, and where they ranked (or didn't) for every service they sold.

**2. The local SEO overhaul.** GBP rebuilt end to end; site structure reorganized around **service + location** searches, so every job they wanted had a page built to rank for it, with metadata matched to how homeowners in their radius actually search.

**3. Content into the gaps.** New pages and content targeting the local keywords competitors had left underserved — the searches with real intent and weak incumbents, where a well-built page can move in weeks instead of months.

**4. The authority campaign.** Off-page work to raise the site's weight: guest posts on relevant higher-authority sites, industry forums and Q&A placements, and tiered link building. This is the slow, unglamorous layer most contractors' SEO never gets.

**5. Video proof on top.** A [customer testimonial film](/case-studies/flow-pros-plumbing) — real customers explaining why they keep coming back — feeding the brand layer while the search layer climbed.

## The result, five months later

By **March 2025**:

- Monthly site traffic: **~1,000 → 136,500+**
- Domain authority (Semrush): **21 → 35**
- Google Business Profile: **leading its 15-mile radius** for core plumbing keywords
- Downstream: a surge in phone calls, form submissions, and booked appointments

## What made it move that fast

Three honest observations from inside the engagement:

**The gaps were real.** Flow Pros' market had high-intent local keywords with genuinely weak competition. Part of SEO skill is picking fights you can win quickly while the longer campaigns compound.

**Everything launched together.** Site structure, GBP, content, and links reinforce each other; run one at a time and each waits on the others. Run them as one sprint and the curve bends early.

**Nothing here was exotic.** Audit, structure, coverage, authority, proof. The differentiator isn't a secret tactic — it's doing all of it, at once, and measuring honestly. That's the same system inside every [TopServ plan](/programs-pricing/pricing).
