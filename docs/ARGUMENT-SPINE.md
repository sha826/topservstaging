# The Argument Spine

*Eight pages. What each one argues, in order. Build from this.*

**To**  Alejandro Munoz, Shahadat Hossain

**From**  Ryan Smith, COO

**Reads with**  Website Build Specification v2.0 and JB’s BrandFormance Messaging and Copy Framework

**Date**  August 2026

> **This is the spine, not the copy. Write over it, do not write around it.**
>
> Every page on this site argues something specific in a specific order. If copy and layout move together without the argument fixed, copy starts getting shaped to fit a section instead of the section getting built to carry the argument. **The result is a good looking site that has stopped arguing.**
>
> With this locked, copy and design run in parallel safely, because both are serving the same thing. **Go build.**

The eight beats come from section 29 of JB’s framework and section 13 of the build spec. Every page below is filled in against them. **Where a beat says something you disagree with, say so and change it. Where it says nothing, that is a gap I could not fill and you should.**

## HOW TO USE THIS

### The positioning hierarchy, on every page

Section 9 of JB’s framework and he calls it extremely important. **Every major page reinforces this order and never inverts it.**

| Level | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Category | BrandFormance. What is it, why does it exist, why is the traditional approach incomplete |
| 2. Outcome | Become the obvious choice. What changes for the contractor |
| 3. Market strategy | Become five mile famous. How familiarity and preference get created |
| 4. System | The BrandFormance method. How TopServ executes it |
| 5. Capabilities | Only now do we name SEO, PPC, LSA, Meta, YouTube, video, OTT, email, reputation, funnels, content, tracking |

**The first impression must never be that TopServ sells SEO and PPC.** It is that TopServ created a different approach to home service marketing called BrandFormance.

### The order of work

**Argument, then structure, then copy and design together.** The first is done, it is above. Alejandro sets structure and SEO against each spine. Then copy and layout run in parallel, both serving a fixed argument.

### The gate before anything reaches Ryan

**The swap test.** Could another home service marketing agency put its logo on this page without substantially changing the copy? If yes, it is not done.

Then five more from JB’s framework. Does the page teach something about BrandFormance. Does the visitor understand why it differs from ordinary digital marketing. Does it explain an outcome rather than list services. Is there evidence near every major claim. Does the visitor know what to do next.

### What to say and what never to say

**Recurring language, used deliberately and not crammed together.** Being found is not the same as being chosen. Performance captures demand, brand creates demand. Own attention, do not just rent it. Become five mile famous. From another option to the obvious choice. Customers buy the company they know before the company they find.

**Never:** unlock your potential, elevate your business, transform your digital presence, innovative digital solutions, results driven marketing, your partner for growth, full service digital marketing. **Those headlines belong to a thousand agencies.** TopServ copy should be recognizable with the logo removed.

No em dashes. Numerals rather than spelled out numbers. Every claim with proof nearby.

### The four grades, worded exactly as the book words them

The public assessment returns one of four. **Use these labels verbatim in every CTA, result screen and piece of copy.** They are the four levels of brand equity from JB’s book, which is the entire reason we can publish them.

| Grade | What the visitor is told |
| --- | --- |
| No Brand Equity | Effectively invisible. Prospects do not recognize the name and pick at random from a list. |
| Name Recognition | They recognize the name but do not care about it. Familiarity without preference. |
| Household Name | Searched for by name. The category search gets skipped entirely. |
| Negative Brand Equity | Actively chosen against. Fix the operation before spending another dollar on marketing. |

**Do not soften No Brand Equity into something friendlier.** A contractor who reads it and recognizes himself is the entire point of the assessment, and the honest label is what makes him book.

### Two places JB’s framework is now stale, so you are not confused when you read it

**His framework says Get Your Brand Score and puts it at step 2.** The spec says Brand Grade at step 3, and the public artifact is a grade rather than a number. **The spec governs.** His document predates that decision.

**His framework suggests /brandformance-method for the Method page.** The spec says /method. **The spec governs.** Everything else in his framework stands and it is the messaging foundation.

### Where this spine is thin

**Page 8 is waiting on which clients can be named.** Placeholders are fine.

**Pages 2 and 3 need the visual explanations more than the copy.** Brand plus performance, the flywheel, five mile famous, rented against owned attention, the customer journey. Those diagrams carry more of the argument than the paragraphs around them. Shaw should start on them now rather than waiting for finished copy.

> **If a beat above is wrong, change it and tell Ryan what you changed and why.**
>
> This is a starting spine built from the doctrine, not a specification you have to defend. **The one thing that cannot move is the order.** Problem, false assumption, insight, principle, proof, plan, outcome, call to action. That sequence is what makes a page argue instead of describe.
