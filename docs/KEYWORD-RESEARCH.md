## 1. HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT (READ FIRST)

1. **Every page has exactly one primary keyword.** Find the page you are building in Section 4, 5, or 6. Use its primary keyword in the title tag, H1, URL (already fixed by the spec), and the first 100 words. Full placement rules are in the SEO Guidelines.
2. **Secondary keywords** are worked into H2s and body copy naturally. Never force all of them. Coverage of the topic beats repetition of the phrase.
3. **Supporting and question keywords** become H2/H3 subheads, FAQ entries, and schema-marked Q&A blocks. These are what win AI citations.
4. **Never assign one keyword to two pages.** The map below is the cannibalization control. If you believe a new page needs a keyword already assigned, the existing page gets strengthened instead, or the conflict goes to Alejandro. Do not silently duplicate.
5. **Positioning outranks SEO.** If a keyword pulls copy toward "we are an SEO/PPC agency," the keyword loses. The site sells BrandFormance, the category. Capabilities are Level 5 in the positioning hierarchy and are never the first impression. SEO gets a page found. The argument on the page does the choosing. That is the company's own thesis and the site must model it.
6. **Intent codes** used below: `C` = commercial (comparing solutions), `T` = transactional (ready to engage), `I` = informational (learning), `N` = navigational (looking for a known thing).

### The two-audience rule

Search around these terms carries two different searchers:

- **The buyer**: a contractor or home service owner looking for marketing help. This is who the site is for.
- **The homeowner**: someone searching "best HVAC system on the market." Never target homeowner intent. Any keyword whose SERP shows consumer results is disqualified regardless of volume.

When in doubt, check the SERP. If the results are marketing agencies and advice for contractors, the keyword is in scope.

## 2. STRATEGY SUMMARY

### Where the demand actually is

Real search demand from contractors clusters in four layers, from most obvious to most valuable:

| Layer | Example terms | Role for TopServ |
|---|---|---|
| **Agency shopping** (C/T) | hvac marketing agency, home services marketing agency, contractor marketing | Money pages. Industries and Programs pages capture this. Highest priority to rank |
| **Tactic education** (I/C) | hvac seo, plumbing marketing ideas, google ads for contractors | Solutions pages and content hub. Enter the conversation, reframe toward BrandFormance |
| **Problem awareness** (I) | how to get hvac leads, lead generation companies for contractors, google ads not working, in house marketing vs agency | Big 5 content. This is where trust is built and bad assumptions are killed |
| **Category and concept** (I) | brand vs performance marketing, brand equity, demand generation vs demand capture, brandformance | The category play. Low volume today, but this is the search language TopServ owns and the AI-citation layer |

### The three-horizon ranking plan

- **Horizon 1 (launch to 90 days):** Own every branded and category term outright (BrandFormance, Five Mile Famous, TopServ Digital, Jonathan Bannister, Home Service Hustle, F#CK Digital Marketing). These are winnable immediately and they are the moat.
- **Horizon 2 (90 days to 12 months):** Rank the Industry pages for trade-specific agency-shopping terms (KD mostly 15 to 35, very winnable for a site with real authority signals) and build the Big 5 content that ranks for problem and cost terms.
- **Horizon 3 (12 months plus):** Compete on the head terms (contractor marketing, hvac marketing, home services marketing agency) which need accumulated topical authority and links, and grow the category terms into real volume. "Brandformance" already shows 170 searches per month at $17.81 CPC. Competitors are already bidding on the category term. Owning its SERP completely is non-negotiable.

### What we deliberately do not chase

Full list with reasons in Section 8. Headlines: no homeowner-intent terms, no commercial construction terms (out of ICP per spec Section 2), no ultra-broad generic terms like "local seo services" (27,100 vol, KD 42) where ranking would position TopServ as a commodity SEO vendor and attract the wrong buyer.
