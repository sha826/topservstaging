## SERP observation

The reported results include a heavy YouTube presence plus articles from contractor and construction marketing publishers. The content package therefore combines a text answer with a video-led angle and gives TopServ a natural path to embed a future companion video.

## Editorial decision

The article is narrowed to residential home service contractors even though the exact keyword can include commercial and general construction intent. This is intentional. TopServ's positioning is stronger when the content qualifies the right buyer instead of chasing the broadest possible traffic.
