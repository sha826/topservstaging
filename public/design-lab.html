The ecosystem hub, animated

The old site's channel diagram — SEO, SEM, GBP, Email, Social Ads, Addressable Geofencing around the TopServ mark — rebuilt as living motion. Four takes, all real-time and GPU-cheap. The real build adds mouse-parallax 3D tilt on top.

D1

Orbital plane

All six channels share one tilted orbit — they swell and brighten as they swing to the front, shrink and dim passing behind the hub. The "3D solar eclipse" look. (Already built as a React component — picking this one is zero extra work.)

D2

The original, alive

Faithful to their diagram's layout — but the nodes float, and energy pulses travel outward from the hub along every spoke. Reads as "the hub powers every channel."

D3

Solar system

Three concentric orbits at different speeds and directions — closest channels orbit fast, outer ones slow. The most "space" of the four; constant gentle motion everywhere.

D4

Carousel wheel

Top-down view: a flat, calm rotation with every channel equally weighted and always fully readable. The most corporate-safe option.
